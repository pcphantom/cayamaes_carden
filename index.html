Her Craft

Made The Old Way
In Sacramento

Nothing here was bought in. The herbs are wild harvested by hand from the land around Sacramento, cut fresh so the aroma and character of the plant are still in the leaf when the infusion begins.

Every oil is warm infused and then cold infused, four to six times over, with fresh plant material each round. It is slow work, and it is how this much plant finds its way into a bottle this small.
